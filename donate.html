Our GoFundMe!

Give Old Electronics a Second Life and Help Kids in Honduras

We are Goatstone Tech, a small startup based in West Chicago, Illinois. We believe old electronics can still make a difference in today's fast-moving technological world. Instead of letting computers collect dust or end up in the trash, we want to give them a second chance and use them to help children get a better education.

Our work currently focuses on retired laptop computers and other electronics. We collect equipment, safely remove personal data for organizations, and refurbish devices that can still be used. Working equipment can then be sold, donated, or reused instead of going to waste!

But our goal goes beyond electronics.

For many reasons, we have a connection to Honduras, and we want to make a difference there. We have chosen to support children in Honduras by helping them access better educational opportunities through Walk Beside Me for Knowledge (WBK), an organization that operates three bilingual schools in major cities across the country.

Through this fundraiser, we hope to help support children's schooling, contribute laptops to school computer labs, and provide musical instruments for children's music programs, all of which WBK accepts!

But... why not donate directly to them? Well, you can! Visit their donation page here: https://wbkhn.org/en/donate.

GoFundMe does not currently allow us to fundraise directly through WBK's website, so we created this fundraiser to help make our mission possible.

We genuinely want to make a difference, and we understand that not everyone has the time or ability to contribute directly. That's why we decided to take action ourselves by collecting retired equipment, with the hope that our efforts can help enrich educational opportunities in another country.

Your donation can help turn something that some organizations consider trash into an opportunity for someone else. Funds raised through this campaign will help Goatstone Tech grow its operations, allowing us to collect, refurbish, and reuse more equipment. As our business grows, we aim to turn that growth into meaningful support for WBK and the children it serves.

So yes... we are also asking for your help.

We want to be transparent about this fundraiser. When you donate, you are supporting Goatstone Tech and our mission to make an impact. We have developed what we call our S.D.R. Vision: Sell, Donate, Reuse. This vision represents the goal we are working towards.

We sell equipment to help fund our project, donate what we can, and reuse what we cannot donate. As a startup working towards becoming an IT asset disposition (ITAD) company focused on supporting schools, we want to recover, recycle, and reuse as much equipment as possible.

So, if we are selling equipment and making profit... why would you donate? Well, lets go deeper... and personal...

Goatstone tech is currently... a garage startup without the garage. So, we are running off of a broken sedan and a corner in the bedroom with a tech bench. We plan to expand! so our true goals for expansion start with this small list;

  • First, 300 squared feet office space rental at $2,709 /YR right in the center of West Chicago!
  • Secondly... a 2015 Ford transit 250 cargo van Medium Roof w/LWB Van 3D at $10,000 we saw on Facebook Marketplace... OR, any vehicle that can help us transport more than our old Toyota corolla...

Yes, that is a lot to ask for. Our way to justify the ask is that we do not need it all at once. We can make multiple trips, call in friends, and fill in all the way to the kitchen until we reach our milestones. We will document every step of the way to show you and show ourselves that positive change can still be achieved.

But hey! there is still profit for us! What about the kids?

Our goal is to set aside at minimum 50% of our profits to support charitable efforts, including our mission to help WBK. By using donations to help expand our operations and committing a significant portion of our profits to giving back, we hope to build a sustainable business that can continue making a difference. As Goatstone Tech grows, we hope to increase our contributions and reach even more children in need.

We also want to keep you informed about the impact of your support. As contributions are made and equipment is delivered, we will share available receipts, school confirmations, and photos of laptops and instruments in their new classrooms whenever possible.

We truly and heartedly are a small startup, but we believe small efforts can make a real difference. Every contribution helps us move closer to a future with less electronic waste and more tools for children to learn.

Thank you for supporting Goatstone Tech and helping us turn technology into opportunity.

P.S. Even if you do not want to support us directly, you can still make a difference by sponsoring a child through WBK: https://wbkhn.org/en/donate
