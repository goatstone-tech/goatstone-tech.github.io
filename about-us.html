About Us

Retired computers, drives, and phones still hold your data; yet many small organizations have nobody assigned to deal with them. Their solution, your solution, might be to put the equipment in a closet forever; or worse! the trash!. All that data is going to become a liability, so we took it upon ourselves to take up the work and take a load off your shoulders.

First, let us be honest. We are a startup. Pickups are local and free when the items qualify. However, we follow the same rules and guidelines that bigger ITAD companies do. We do it under a written policy, with verification and validation on every job and a certificate.

And the equipment we don't destroy? Check out our S.D.R Vision! (not to be confused with standard dynamic range...)

How we work

Equipment is logged at before, during, and after pick-up. We test and sanitize it. Any working equipment goes back for resale, but things with no resale value go to named downstream recycling partners; and we ask for receipts.

About certification...

Well, turns out NIST does not certify companies or software. When we say our sanitization follows NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, our word is truly all you can take as evidence. It is basically defined by the written policy, the job log, and the certificate for each drive. The details are on our data destruction page if you wish to know more about all that.

So, no, no real certifications; but we don't need one with the way we work.
