How to Recycle Ink Locally

We cannot take ink or alkaline batteries, so remove them before pickup. Here is where to take empty ink cartridges around West Chicago instead of putting them in the trash. Programs change, so confirm before you make a special trip.

Target. Some Target locations have recycling stations near the entrance that take ink cartridges. Call your local store first to confirm it takes them.

Staples. Takes empty ink and toner cartridges in store, free. Rewards members can earn points for cartridges they drop off.

Best Buy. Free drop-off for ink and toner cartridges near Customer Service. Daily limits apply.

Office Depot and OfficeMax. Take empty ink and toner cartridges in store, with rewards for members.

Manufacturer mail-back. HP Planet Partners, Canon, Epson, and Brother offer free prepaid labels or envelopes to mail empty cartridges back.

SCARCE, Glen Ellyn. Accepts inkjet cartridges for reuse and recycling. No toners.

Do not put ink cartridges in curbside recycling. Bag them so they do not leak, and take them to one of the drop-offs above.
