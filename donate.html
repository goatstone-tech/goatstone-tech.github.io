Our goal

We want to help you! but our objective is to give electronics a second chance while allowing us the possibility to donate at least 50% of our profits to help children in central America!

Donate

Thank you for wanting to help. Donations keep this project going: equipment that still has life left gets refurbished and then sold, donated, or repurposed, and funds help us donate to non-profits that benefit children.

Donate equipment

Email us at goatstone-tech@outlook.com and tell us what you have and where it is. We reply with a pickup plan, the same way as a regular pickup.

We also take musical instruments. They go to children's music programs in Honduras.

Donate funds

There is no online payment form yet. Email us at goatstone-tech@outlook.com and tell us you would like to donate funds, and we will reply with how to do it.

Donating reaches further than the device.

Some laptops will go to the computer lab at WBK's schools in Honduras. Some of the money from resales will sponsor a child's school year. We sponsor via money, laptops, and musical instruments.

Money donated to Goatstone Tech goes to the work: pickups, sanitization, and refurbishing.

See where it goes on the S.D.R Vision page.

Visit WBK: https://wbkhn.org/en
