Sell, Donate, Reuse

Sell. To put simply and transparently, we sell valuable equipment to fund this very project.

Donate. Equipment that can be used for educational purposes, such as laptops or tablets, gets donated to non-profit institutions inside or outside the United States. We hope to make a change in children's lives and educate the youth about technology and recycling for a better future.

Even if no equipment qualifies, we donate extra funds monthly to non-profits that benefit children across the globe, or that align with our goals of keeping electronics out of the trash.

Reuse. Just as we sell to fund our project, we also repurpose what we can to improve our service. From office equipment to anything to aid on the go.
