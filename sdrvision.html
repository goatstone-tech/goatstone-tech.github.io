S.D.R Vision

Recovered units are graded and listed through resale channels, one at a time and in wholesale lots. Each unit ships with its sanitization record, tied to its serial number.

For buyers

Wholesale lots of business-class desktops and laptops are the standard offer; lot composition, grades, and per-unit pricing are published with each lot. Every drive in every unit has been purged or replaced and certified. Ask for the current lot list.

For sellers

S.D.R Vision is where liquidation value comes from. Equipment with resale value offsets free pickup and certified destruction on the rest of your lot; equipment without it is recycled downstream at no landfill cost to you.

Learn more about selling, donating, and reuse.

Asset Recovery

Working equipment goes back into service. We test what you retire, sanitize it to NIST Rev. 2, and return it to the market with its paperwork in order.

The reuse loop

Every unit is documented at intake, tested (power, screen, battery operation, storage health), sanitized to NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, cleaned, and prepared for resale. Units that fail testing are harvested for parts where sensible; the rest goes to downstream recycling with a receipt.

What recovers well

Business-class laptops and desktops (Dell Latitude, Lenovo ThinkPad, HP EliteBook class), recent phones and tablets, and working monitors. Consumer gear is taken for recycling; recovery value concentrates in business hardware.

Why it matters

A working machine has resale value; a shredded one has scrap value. Reuse pays better and wastes less. The certificate chain shows the data was sanitized before the unit went back out.

Where it goes

Some of the laptops we refurbish will go to the computer lab at WBK's schools in Honduras. WBK is Walk Beside Me for Knowledge, and it runs three bilingual schools in Villanueva, El Progreso and San Pedro Sula. The students will use the computers to learn the digital skills their future studies and jobs will need.

Some of the money from resales will sponsor a child's school year. We sponsor via money, laptops, and musical instruments.

We will show you what happens. The receipts, the scholarship confirmations, and photos of the laptops in their new classroom will be posted here as they come in.
