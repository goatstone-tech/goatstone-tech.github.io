FAQ

What does it cost? Local pickup and certified data cleanup are free for standard lots. We keep the resale value of the hardware when it applies. Click here to learn more about our S.D.R. goals.

Unusual lots, such as IT Asset Disposition and Liquidation of datacenters or large volumes, are quoted per lot first. Due to our current company size and availability, we cannot promise we will be able to assist you in large lots.

How do I know my data is gone? Every drive gets a certificate with its serial number, the method used, and the validator's signature, following the NIST Rev. 2 Sec. 4.6 field list. Click here to learn more about it!

What does not qualify? Printers, microwaves, large appliances over 30 lbs, audio equipment, large CRT screens. Not sure? Ask!

Do you take musical instruments? Yes. They go to children's music programs in Honduras.

Ink and alkaline batteries must be removed before pickup due to handling. Need to recycle ink? Learn how to!
